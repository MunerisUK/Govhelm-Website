News

Build notes, architectural decisions and integration milestones. GovHelm is pre-release, so this is the record of what has been built rather than of what has been shipped to a live service — dated releases begin at 1.0.

Platform 4 September 2026

Identity resolution, made real

The identity resolver lands: any identifier a jurisdiction accepts — a social security number, a registration card, a passport or national identity card from any issuing state, a local driving licence — now resolves to the one opaque internal key that joins domains. The key is never an issued number, so a leaked passport number unlocks nothing.

Resolution is governed rather than convenient. A tenant resolves only the schemes its profile accepts, an unaccepted scheme is denied outright, and every attempt is written to the audit log — because looking a person up is an access. It is also the first module born in TypeScript, so the minimisation contract is checked at compile time.

Integration 27 August 2026

A published contract for finance, payroll and HR

The integration definition for CivitaOS as an external finance, payroll and HR system of record is published, with a conformance checklist as the acceptance gate. It sets out the connector contract, the per-domain claim catalogues, the agreements, the outbound instructions, eventing, residency and branding.

First on the list of non-negotiables: minimisation across the seam. Claims, never raw figures. Salary, pay band and bank details stay inside the counterparty system, and an integration that breaks that invariant is not accepted.

Transparency 6 August 2026

The rules are now open data

Each tenant publishes its data-sharing agreement register and its capability catalogue as DCAT JSON-LD, with plain-JSON distributions. Anyone can now audit the rules that gate access to citizen data without seeing any citizen data.

The invariant is enforced by construction: the record, the claims, the figures and the audit entries are absent from that layer entirely. The minimisation guarantee that keeps the record closed is exactly what makes the metadata safe to open.

Integration 16 July 2026

HR and payroll join the seam

Government is also a large employer. The HR and payroll connector returns employment claims — is a government employee, employment active — and never salary or pay band, and forwards a leaver instruction when a death is registered.

It composes with the event backbone rather than being wired to it: a death registered at the authoritative source publishes a fact, and the connector reacts. Neither the producer nor the consumer knows about the other.

Integration 25 June 2026

Finance and ERP, both directions

The finance connector brokers an external ERP two ways, both governed exactly like an in-app access: minimised claims about a citizen’s finance account on the way in, and outbound payment or posting instructions on the way out, receipted so both ends can reconcile.

Raw amounts stay in the finance system. That is not a configuration choice; there is no agreement that returns them.

Platform 4 June 2026

Durable persistence behind a seam

The domain logic now runs unchanged over the in-memory store or over Postgres, with the storage behind a repository seam and a migration job that runs before the application starts.

One database per government, not one database with a tenant column. Isolation is structural, and the readiness probe covers the backend so a tenancy whose database is unreachable does not take traffic.

Delivery 14 May 2026

One image, one tenancy, one pinned digest

The deployment layer lands: a tenant-agnostic chart with no jurisdiction in any template, per-government descriptors carrying the entire surface that varies, and GitOps promotion by immutable digest.

The guard is the part that matters. A production descriptor may only run a digest already proven in that same government’s test ring — another government’s successful test does not count — and rollback is reverting the pin.

Platform 23 April 2026

Proactive government, demonstrated

Revenue gains the write side and the event backbone goes in behind it. Filing income is an authoritative write at the source that publishes an event carrying a reference, never the figure.

Benefits is subscribed, so entitlement re-assesses itself and the change appears in the citizen’s audit log — without the citizen opening the benefits page or filling in a form. That is the difference between proactive government as a slogan and as a test.

What is not here

There are no customer announcements, no go-live notices and no case studies, because there are none to report. When the first government runs a live service on the platform, that will be the entry that matters and it will say so plainly.
