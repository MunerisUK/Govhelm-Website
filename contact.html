Company

Contact

Five ways in, depending on what you need. A demonstration is run against your own jurisdiction profile rather than a script, so the first conversation is usually about your constitution rather than our features.

Demonstrations and pricing

An hour, run against a profile written from your jurisdiction: your constitutional facts, your capability toggles, your agreements, your logo. Pricing is given on that call rather than after three meetings.

Evaluation environments

A deployment of your own with sample data and your profile loaded, so you can register a death, file an income, watch entitlement recompute and read the audit log as a citizen would.

Security reports

Handled ahead of everything else. If you believe you have found a vulnerability, please report it before disclosing it; a coordinated disclosure policy is published alongside the first production deployment.

Accessibility problems

If any part of this site or the platform is difficult or impossible to use, we want the report. Accessibility defects are treated as defects, not as enhancement requests.

Partner integrations

If you run a government system of record — finance, payroll, HR, registries, clinical care — the connector seam is documented and the conformance checklist is published. Integration definitions are written before either side writes code.

Procurement and assurance

For due diligence: the architecture pack, the decision records, the security position including its gaps, source review under non-disclosure, and the exit terms in writing.

Contact details are not published on this site yet

The routes above describe how to reach us; the addresses and numbers behind them are being finalised ahead of the first production deployment, and publishing a channel nobody is monitoring would be worse than publishing none.

If you have arrived here from a conversation with us, use the address you already have — it reaches the same people.

What to bring

A first conversation that is worth an hour

The demonstrations that go well are the ones where somebody brings the thing they are sure will not fit: a fused judicial role, a parish tier, a residency regime that gates work rights, a currency at par with another, a system nobody is allowed to touch, a regulator with a firm view.

Those are the cases the profile schema was written from, and where the architecture does not cope, it is more useful for both of us to find out in the first hour than in the fourth month.

Useful to have to hand

  • Your departmental structure and who owns which data domain
  • The identity schemes your jurisdiction accepts, and who issues them
  • Your data-protection regime and your regulator’s posture
  • The systems of record you are not going to replace
  • The one service that would prove the case internally
